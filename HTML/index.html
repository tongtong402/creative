BERYL'S CHOCOLATE

Beryl's Chocolate and Confectionery Sdn.Bhd. is a Malaysian chocolate manufacturer founded by Ting Sii Liong in 1995.

The company established its first factory in Desa Serdang which is producing consumer-packed chocolates that were mainly sold through duty-free shops and supermarkets across Malaysia.

In 1998, Beryl's moved to a new factory in the Seri Kembangan Industrial Area and expanded its production lines to offer a wider range of high-quality chocolate products.
